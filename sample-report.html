How to read Diagnostic and Synthesis results

Monderman has four Diagnostics: Operational Systems, Decision Velocity, Structural Clarity, and Institutional Performance, plus two Synthesis products: Depth Synthesis and Cross-Lens Synthesis. Each Diagnostic remains a distinct measurement. Eligible Included runs can later support Synthesis in Analysis.

The four Diagnostic tabs preserve the current executive-report hierarchy for one Diagnostic lens: condition, dimensions, bounded burden, governance implications, participant evidence, remedies, evidence basis, and interpretation limits. The Synthesis tabs preserve the current evidence rules: Depth Synthesis publishes the Median Diagnostic Score and distribution from eligible same-Diagnostic runs; Cross-Lens Synthesis keeps every lens separate and publishes a Cross-Lens Composite Score only when the declared coherence controls pass. Otherwise the Composite Score is withheld with the exact reason and the evidence that could unlock it.

Representative product outputs, not customer data. The organizations, populations, scenarios, and values are illustrative. They show the kinds of outputs, evidence limits, visuals, and recommendations the current products can present; actual customer results depend on the answers, context, eligible run set, and evidence available at the time.
