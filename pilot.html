The pilot

One unit. One defined scope. Thirty days.

The Pattern Pilot is for an organization that wants to compare a small group's responses before beginning a paid subscription. Six to twelve people complete the same Diagnostic about one decision process, workflow, or organizational unit. Monderman keeps differences between roles and responses visible.

6 to 12 peopleA small participant group from one organizational unit.
30 daysA defined period to collect and review the group's responses.
No automatic chargeNo card is required, and the pilot does not renew automatically.

What you receive

Individual reports and a combined view of the group.

The pilot includes the Pattern Workspace, participant campaigns, Executive Reports, an Action Plan, and Depth Synthesis when the completed runs are suitable for combination. Depth Synthesis shows the median score, distribution, coverage, and differences between participant perspectives. It describes the submitted group, not the entire organization.

AI-assisted interpretation is currently enabled for eligible Diagnostic and Synthesis reports, including pilot reports. Monderman's code calculates scores; Anthropic's Claude selects, prioritizes and reviews suitable explanations and actions from approved material. It cannot change scores or freely write recommendations.

Selected answers, results and context go to Anthropic; Synthesis uses group results. Optional written observations are not currently included in AI interpretation. Customer content is not used for model training. Outside processing is not zero retention. See the Privacy Notice.

While interpretation is pending or unavailable, sponsors can reopen saved reports in the Workspace later without asking participants to answer again. Older reports are not automatically regenerated or replaced. Review suggestions before acting.
