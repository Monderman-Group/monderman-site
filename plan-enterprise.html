  • Bespoke Diagnostic design for your structure on DAII, with calibration, validation, and comparability requirements defined explicitly before any custom instrument is used built for you
  • Unlimited analyst and admin workspace users, where Pattern includes five analyst and two admin workspace users
  • Enterprise procurement planning: security review inputs, counsel-reviewed DPA preparation where required, purchase-order requirements, and custom contract scoping subject to a separately executed agreement

Terms, in plain language

These are summary terms, not the binding agreement. They are provided so you know what to expect. Your subscription is governed by the order form or services agreement Monderman provides. We recommend your legal or contracts team review all terms before you sign.
What you are buying
An Enterprise subscription with everything in Pattern, unlimited participant responses, Syntheses, self-runs, and analyst and admin workspace users, enterprise procurement and data terms, and access to separately scoped bespoke Diagnostic or participant-perspective design. Expert interpretation requires a separate written scope unless expressly included in the order.
Billing & term
Custom, scoped on your services agreement and invoiced on your paper: purchase order, services agreement, or the contract vehicle you already use. Term as agreed.
Participant responses, Syntheses & workspace users
No per-participant pricing. Completed participant responses, Depth Synthesis, Cross-Lens Synthesis, self-runs, and analyst and admin workspace users are unlimited.
Scoped terms
Pricing, procurement and data terms, multi-entity configuration, and any bespoke Diagnostic or participant-perspective design are stated in the order form.
Support
Priority platform support with a named contact, so escalation does not start from scratch each time. Platform support covers using the software: running diagnostics, exports, and access. Interpreting your results is separate from the subscription and requires a written scope.
Your data
Your Diagnostic inputs and results are used to provide and support Monderman. Monderman does not sell personal information or use customer data for advertising. The basis behind every score is published with the Executive Report. Current processing and providers are described in the Privacy Notice and subprocessor page. Any customer-specific DPA, retention, or residency commitment must be set out in a separately executed agreement.
Beta status
Monderman is in active beta. It is in production use and improving on a known cadence. Your tier includes a direct feedback channel and hands-on correction where a structural edge case affects your result. You are an early customer of a product that is still maturing, not a buyer of frozen software.
Cancellation
As agreed on your services agreement: Enterprise terms are written with your contracts team, not imposed. Non-renewing by default unless your agreement says otherwise.
