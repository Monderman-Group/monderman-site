A shared understanding

One organization.
Many perspectives.

People doing the work, managers and senior leaders can describe the same work differently. A responsibility that seems clear to one person may be uncertain to another. A decision that appears straightforward may pass through several reviews before anything happens.

Q²-SODI gives those perspectives a common starting point. Each person answers a structured questionnaire. The software brings the findings into clear reports, helping you ask better questions, identify priorities and make informed choices about improvement.

Qualitative perspectives. Quantitative results. The two Qs stand for qualitative and quantitative: how your people describe the work, together with structured numerical results that help you see patterns and compare over time. SODI names the four diagnostics: Structural Clarity, Operational Systems, Decision Velocity and Institutional Performance.

Start with a team, department, process or decision path. Extend the view across your organization as the work develops.
