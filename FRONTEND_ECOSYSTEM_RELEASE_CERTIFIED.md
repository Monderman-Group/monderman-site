# Six-product frontend ecosystem release

Certified frontend integration for the Monderman six-product ecosystem.

- Four Diagnostics: Structural Clarity, Decision Velocity, Operational Systems, Institutional Performance
- Two Syntheses: Depth Synthesis, Cross-Lens Synthesis
- Canonical participant vantages: Operational, Managerial, Senior Leader
- Canonical lifecycle: Staged -> Reviewed -> Included
- Depth Synthesis reports a Median Diagnostic Score
- Cross-Lens Composite Score is evidence-gated; withheld outputs state why and how to unlock a composite
- Workspace Analysis is the parent section for Synthesis
- Pricing language uses no per-participant pricing
- Signal: 50 completed participant responses/year and 12 Syntheses/year
- Pattern: 500 completed participant responses/year, unlimited Syntheses, anonymous participant responses
- Enterprise: platform ceilings removed; bespoke vantage design may be separately scoped
