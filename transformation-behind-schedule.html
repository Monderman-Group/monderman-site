Decision Velocity use case

Transformation behind schedule

Examine whether decisions and approvals are contributing to repeated delay.

The problem

Test whether repeated delays share an operating pattern.

A delayed milestone may have a local cause such as a vendor, a hiring gap, or a dependency. When similar delays appear across several workstreams, leaders may also need to examine how approvals, escalation, and handoffs affect the program.

Begin with one decision

Examine one defined decision path.

In about ten minutes, one leader answers structured questions about a specific decision. The run produces a Decision Velocity score and result band. Your invited Workspace saves the Executive Report, which describes the conditions reported in that scope.

One person’s result describes that run. It is not an organization-wide finding.

What a campaign adds

Compare reports across workstreams.

Invite six to twelve people from the affected workstreams. Depth Synthesis summarizes eligible runs, including the median, spread, and differences between operational and steering-group responses. The summary can show where further review is warranted without assuming one cause.

What comes after

Make a change, then repeat the same measure.

Run the same version for the same decision and participant group in about 90 days. A score difference means the reported responses changed. It does not, by itself, explain why they changed or prove that an intervention caused the difference. Repeat measurement is available through a subscription and is not required to begin.
