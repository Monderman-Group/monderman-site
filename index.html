Our Approach

Four lenses on the same system.

Four diagnostic lenses on the same institutional system: time through it, legibility of it, weight of it, and whether it still holds. Together, they read where strategy becomes execution, or stalls under the weight of unclear structures, slower decisions, and administrative systems that no longer support the work.

Measurement you can verify

A prompt. A judgment. An instrument.

There are three ways to ask what your bureaucracy is costing you. A general AI chatbot gives a thoughtful answer that changes every time you ask. A traditional study renders a considered judgment once. Monderman is the third kind: a calibrated instrument with deterministic scoring and compatible re-measurement, so a later read can show whether the measured condition changed.

A promptUseful for thinking. Ask twice, receive two different reads: no baseline, no quantification, nothing defensible to a board.
A judgmentExperienced and bespoke, but a one-time event at deployment economics. Drift after the binder lands goes unmeasured.
An instrumentWithin the same instrument/scorer version, the same structured answers produce the same score. What the instrument measures

Experience is evidence. The system is the diagnosis.

Reported experience matters, but it cannot by itself identify the organizational mechanisms producing that experience. Monderman measures the operating layer beneath the signal.

Sentiment measurement

Locates reported strain.

Engagement, morale, confidence, and perceived experience show where people feel pressure or disconnection.

From first read to governed change

Built for the second read, not just the first.

Monderman turns a one-time diagnosis into a disciplined measurement cycle. Compatible evidence shows what changed, what did not, and whether the recovery hypothesis became more or less credible.

01 · DIAGNOSESee the system.

→
02 · BASELINESave the evidence.

03 · ACTChange the mechanism.

Use the evidence to target ownership, authority, routing, process load, or performance discipline.

→

Run a compatible later read to see whether the measured condition changed and strengthen or weaken the recovery hypothesis.
