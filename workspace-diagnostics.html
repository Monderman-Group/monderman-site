Diagnostic campaigns

Invite participants into a bounded operating pathway

Create a Diagnostic campaign, choose the participant vantage, and send each participant a unique invitation. Completed participant runs are Staged for review before they can be Included in analysis.

Composing campaigns is an admin action

Your role can view campaign tracking below, but only organization admins can compose and send. Ask an admin to grant access if you need it.
