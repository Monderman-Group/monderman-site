Two-Tenant Isolation Harness

Creates a real second tenant with real data, then attempts to reach it from your account and attempts to reach your account from it. Isolation is tested in both directions, which is stronger than testing one way. No email round-trip: the second tenant is created with a session issued directly by Supabase.

This leaves something behind, deliberately. A second organization and its user account persist after the run, because a browser client cannot delete an organization or an auth user. Both are named so they are obvious, and the marker rows the harness writes are deleted. Cleanup SQL is printed at the end and included in the downloaded JSON. Keeping tenant B is also useful: every future run can reuse it instead of creating another.
