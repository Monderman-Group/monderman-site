Pattern · 30-day Beta trial

Use the full Pattern Workspace for 30 days.

A one-time beta evaluation for an eligible account whose Workspace is currently on the standard Trial level. No card is required to start, and the trial does not renew automatically.

  • All four Diagnostics, standard depths and participant perspectives
  • Diagnostic campaigns and up to 500 participant responses
  • Unlimited Depth and Cross-Lens Synthesis
  • Anonymous participant responses
  • Pattern Workspace capacity for five analysts and two admins
What happens on day 30: the Pattern trial ends automatically. If you have not explicitly chosen a paid subscription, nothing is charged and your Workspace returns to the standard Trial level. Your saved work is retained. Standard Trial access limits apply after day 30; choosing a paid plan restores the applicable Pattern access.

One Pattern trial per eligible account identity. Deleting or replacing a Workspace does not reset eligibility. An organization admin must start the trial.
