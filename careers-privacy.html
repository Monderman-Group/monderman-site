Monderman, LLC uses the information you choose to share to consider you for possible future work. The roles on our Careers page are not current vacancies. Sharing your details does not guarantee an interview, a response or employment.

What you provide

The future-interest form asks for your name, email, U.S. state or DC where you would perform the work, role interests and résumé PDF. You may also provide a professional profile or portfolio link, a short message and an optional cover letter PDF. The service records a submission reference and information needed to protect the form from abuse.

Please do not include Social Security numbers, dates of birth, identity documents, financial account details or medical information. We do not ask for salary history, citizenship, immigration documents or demographic monitoring information in this form.

How we use it

We use these details to review relevant capabilities, consider possible future roles, contact you about that interest and administer the submission. An acknowledgement confirms receipt only; it is not a hiring decision. Registering interest does not subscribe you to a marketing campaign.

Documents and service providers

Résumé and cover-letter documents are intended for restricted recruiting access, not public download links. Supabase infrastructure is used for private application records and documents, Resend for transactional receipt emails, Cloudflare for form-abuse protection, and Google Workspace for the owner’s recruiting inbox. Jason may receive résumé and cover-letter attachments in a recruiting notification. Applicant acknowledgement emails do not include those attachments. Access is limited to recruiting and administration; these details are not used for AI ranking, organizational diagnostics or marketing. See our service-provider information and Privacy Policy for broader context.

How long records may be kept

We actively consider future-interest submissions for 12 months from submission, without automatic renewal. After withdrawal or expiry, we normally delete your submitted materials and retain only a limited administrative record for up to four years from submission. We retain information longer only where required for legal obligations or a legal hold. Information used in actual recruitment follows the applicable recruitment retention schedule. Deletion is reviewed and performed manually, including recruiting copies in the owner’s email inbox; this is not an automated deletion process.

Your choices and questions

You can request access to or correction of your information, withdraw from future-role consideration, request deletion or exercise applicable privacy rights by contacting jason.adamson@monderman.com. We may need enough information to identify the relevant record. Withdrawal from consideration and deletion are different: legal recordkeeping obligations or a legal hold may limit deletion.

For an accommodation or an alternative way to share your interest, contact Jason at the same address. Please do not send sensitive medical information in an initial message.

The future-interest form is separate from Monderman’s product evaluations. No account or purchase is needed. Registering interest does not enroll you in a product evaluation or a marketing list.
