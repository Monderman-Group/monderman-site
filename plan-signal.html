What this tier includes

Signal is built for a team. It includes all four Diagnostics, all standard depths and participant perspectives, unlimited self-runs, 50 completed participant responses a year through Diagnostic campaigns, and 12 Syntheses a year. Depth Synthesis summarizes eligible Included runs from one Diagnostic. Cross-Lens Synthesis compares different Diagnostics and publishes a Composite Score only when the evidence is sufficiently coherent. When you need 500 participant responses, unlimited Syntheses, or anonymous participant responses, Pattern is the next tier.

Terms, in plain language

These are summary terms, not the binding agreement. They are provided so you know what to expect. Your subscription is governed by the order form or services agreement Monderman provides. We recommend your legal or contracts team review all terms before you sign.
What you are buying
A subscription to all four Diagnostics at every depth and standard participant perspective, unlimited self-runs, 50 completed participant responses a year, 12 Syntheses a year across Depth Synthesis and Cross-Lens Synthesis, saved history and Analysis, two analyst workspace users and one admin workspace user for the term. Anonymous participant responses and unlimited Syntheses are part of Pattern. Expert interpretation is not included at this tier.
Billing & term
$1,000 per month, billed monthly by card; or $950/month billed quarterly (save 5%); or $850/month billed annually (save 15%). Checkout and card handling by Stripe: card details never touch Monderman. Invoicing on a purchase order is available via an order form.
Participant responses & Syntheses
No per-participant pricing. 50 completed participant responses a year, counted when an invited participant completes a Diagnostic. Unlimited self-runs do not draw on that allowance. 12 Syntheses a year. Two analyst workspace users and one admin workspace user.
The allowances
Self-runs are unlimited. The 50 participant-response allowance and 12-Synthesis allowance are annual and reset annually from the start of the paid allowance period, independent of whether billing is monthly, quarterly, or annual.
Support
Platform support by email, aiming to respond within two business days. Response times are an aim, not a guarantee. Platform support covers using the software: running diagnostics, exports, and access. Interpreting your results is a separate, scoped engagement rather than part of the subscription.
Your data
Your Diagnostic inputs and results are used to provide and support Monderman. Monderman does not sell personal information or use customer data for advertising. The basis behind every score is published with the Executive Report. Current processing and providers are described in the Privacy Notice and subprocessor page. Any customer-specific DPA, retention, or residency commitment must be set out in a separately executed agreement.
Beta status
Monderman is in active beta. It is in production use and improving on a known cadence. Your tier includes a direct feedback channel and hands-on correction where a structural edge case affects your result. You are an early customer of a product suite that is still maturing, not a buyer of frozen software.
Cancellation
Renews automatically each billing period. Cancel any time from your workspace billing portal: the change takes effect at the end of the period already paid. No multi-year lock-in.
