Data quality
How much of this can you trust?

Screening flags responses that look careless or inconsistent — internal contradictions, duplicate submissions, very low self-rated confidence, thin coverage, and (once you have ~30 reads of one instrument) statistical outliers. Flagged runs are set aside from group averages; nothing is deleted, and an admin can re-include any run. It’s most meaningful once a cohort is large enough to compare against.

Over time

How has it moved?

Repeated reads on the same instrument, plotted by when they were taken. The line is observed history — not a forecast.

A trend needs two or more Included runs from the same Diagnostic on the same unit and compatible instrument and scorer versions. Incompatible runs are kept separate rather than compared.

Across units

Where does it sit, unit by unit?

The most recent read of one instrument, compared across the business units that have taken it.

Latest Diagnostic snapshot

What is the latest included result from each Diagnostic?

This is a side-by-side snapshot, not a Synthesis. Each card keeps its own scope and date; do not read the four scores as one organization-wide condition.

Across vantages

Do participant vantages differ on a comparable pathway?

Operational, Managerial, and Senior Leader results appear together only when Diagnostic version and operating-pathway scope are compatible.

Calibration position

What calibration position did each Diagnostic report?

These are the Diagnostic's reported design-reference positions. Monderman does not convert them into empirical peer percentiles unless a separate peer dataset supports that claim.

Before-and-after change

What changed after the intervention?

Uses an explicitly linked before result and later like-for-like remeasurement. The displayed delta is observed; it is not attributed causally to the intervention.

A pair appears only after an Action Plan links a source Diagnostic or Synthesis to a later like-for-like remeasurement.

Syntheses

Build a Depth Synthesis or Cross-Lens Synthesis

Depth Synthesis combines two or more eligible runs from one Diagnostic. Cross-Lens Synthesis compares eligible runs from two or more Diagnostics. A Cross-Lens Composite Score is published only when the evidence is sufficiently coherent.

The API verifies unique source runs, organization scope, measurement window, instrument versions, participant depth, and lens balance. If a Cross-Lens Composite Score is withheld, the report states why and what actions could unlock one.
