Advisory services · scope & enquire

The software tells you what is happening. Sometimes you want someone who has fixed it before.

Monderman’s diagnostics are built to be read without help, and most organizations should use them that way. Advisory exists for the cases where the finding is uncomfortable, contested, or structural enough that leadership wants a practitioner in the room rather than a report.

This is not a tier.

Every Monderman subscription is software. It does not include people, hours, or judgment, and the price does not change if you want them. Advisory is contracted separately, scoped to a specific piece of work, with a fixed fee and a fixed end. That separation is deliberate: it keeps the software honestly priced for the organizations that will never need us in the room, and it keeps an engagement from quietly becoming a retainer.

Fixed scope, fixed feeEach engagement below has a defined deliverable and a defined end. No hourly billing, no open-ended retainer, no scope that grows while you are looking away.
Deliberately limitedA small number of engagements each year. This is one practitioner’s attention, not a bench, and pretending otherwise would be the first broken promise.
Your data stays yoursAdvisory reads the results you already own. Nothing about an engagement changes what the platform stores, who can see it, or the anonymity you promised your people.

The engagements

Three shapes, in ascending order of how much of the problem you want handed over. Each assumes you already have diagnostic results; if you do not, start with the software and come back when you have something to read.

Calibration review

from $15,000 one engagement

Two to three weeks · remote · one pathway or one population read

You have run a diagnostic, or sent one to a group, and the result is either surprising or contested. A calibration review is a senior second read: what the numbers support, what they do not, which competing interpretation the evidence actually favours, and what would have to be true for the uncomfortable reading to be wrong.

  • A written second read of your results, including where we disagree with the automated interpretation
  • An explicit account of the competing explanations and what would settle between them
  • The three or four moves the evidence genuinely supports, in order
  • One working session with whoever has to act on it
What this is not. It is not an implementation plan and it does not produce new measurement. It tells you what your existing results mean and what to do about them.

Pathway redesign

from $45,000 one engagement

One quarter · remote with optional onsite · one pathway, end to end

One pathway is measurably costing you capacity and you want it structurally fixed, not documented. This takes a single approval chain, decision, or handoff sequence from diagnosis through redesign to re-measurement, working with the people who own it rather than around them.

  • Diagnosis across vantages, so the redesign answers what the front line sees and not only what leadership believes
  • A redesigned structure: ownership, decision rights, and handoff boundaries specified to the point of being actionable
  • Working sessions with the people who hold the pathway, because a design nobody agreed to does not survive contact
  • Re-measurement at the end on the same instrument, so recovery is demonstrated rather than asserted
What this is not. It is not organization-wide transformation and it is not change management. One pathway, properly fixed, with evidence it held.

Executive readout

from $7,500 one engagement

One to two weeks · remote or onsite · one board or leadership session

The finding is clear enough and the difficulty is the room. A readout puts the evidence in front of a board or leadership team with someone independent presenting it, which is often the difference between a result being absorbed and a result being managed away.

  • A board-ready presentation of the evidence, written for people who will not read a diagnostic report
  • The session itself, presented independently, including the questions we expect and how the evidence answers them
  • A short written record of what was decided and what was contested
Why independence matters here. Findings about structure are frequently findings about someone in the room. An outside presenter can say the difficult part without inheriting the politics of having said it.
Anything else is scoped from scratch. Bespoke instrument design, multi-pathway programmes, and government work with security or contracting requirements are quoted against the actual scope. If what you need is not one of the three above, say what the problem is rather than which engagement you think fits.

Terms, plainly

What you are buying
A named engagement with a defined deliverable and a defined end date, delivered by a senior practitioner. Advisory is a professional services agreement, separate from any software subscription, and buying it does not change your subscription or its price.
What it is not
Not a retainer, not an hourly arrangement, and not included in Signal, Pattern, or Enterprise. No tier grants advisory time, and no amount of subscription spend converts into it.
Prerequisites
Calibration review and executive readout require existing diagnostic results. Pathway redesign works best where a population read exists, so the redesign answers more than one vantage; where it does not, the engagement begins by producing one.
Fees & scheduling
Fixed fee per engagement, quoted in writing before work begins, with the deliverable and end date named in the same document. Fees start at the figures above and rise with scope, not with hours. Engagements are scheduled in sequence, so start dates depend on what is already booked.
Capacity
A small number of engagements each year, deliberately. If the calendar is full we will say so and tell you when it is not, rather than accepting work we cannot do properly.
Confidentiality & your data
Engagements work from results you already hold. An engagement does not alter platform access, retention, or the anonymity commitments made to people who took a diagnostic; where responses were collected anonymously, they remain anonymous to us as well, because the system stores no link back to the person.
Independence
Advisory reads what the instruments produced. We will tell you where we think the automated interpretation is wrong, and where the evidence does not support a conclusion you would prefer.
