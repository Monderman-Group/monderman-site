The blind spot

Most institutions can see outcomes. Fewer can see the administrative conditions beneath them.

Administrative reality sits underneath visible performance: how legible the structure is, how decisions move, how much operating burden the system carries, and whether performance still holds under load.

The measurement architecture

One path from context to a saved baseline.

Structured answers determine the score. In Interview mode, AI may phrase questions and code free-text replies into the instrument's existing structured answer options; the scoring engine itself is deterministic. AI may also assist with post-score interpretation, but it does not calculate or set the score.
