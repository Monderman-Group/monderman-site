What may explain it

Possible explanations to investigate, not established causes.

One operational account reports that when a supplier needs an exception, procurement and the requesting team sometimes each expect the other to decide, and that this participant's experience is mainly routine requests. A senior leader account reports hearing different accounts about who decides exceptions, with their own experience mainly the escalated ones. Together these reported conditions could mean that contested ownership sits in exception handling rather than routine onboarding. A practical check is to pull a set of recent exception cases and record who made the final decision in each.
