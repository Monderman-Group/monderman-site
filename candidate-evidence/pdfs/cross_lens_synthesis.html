What may explain it

Possible explanations to investigate, not established causes.

One manager account states that team members disagree about whether duplicate entry between the tracker and the vendor system is the main difficulty or whether unclear exceptions create most of the rework, and that the account covers both routine requests and exceptions. An operational account says that for a supplier exception, procurement and the requesting team sometimes each expect the other to decide, while a colleague considers the written responsibility table sufficient. It is possible that difficulty concentrates in exception cases rather than routine files, though one manager account also reports that ownership is clear in practice for some team members. A practical check is to pull a set of recent exception files and recent routine files and compare who actually decided each one.
